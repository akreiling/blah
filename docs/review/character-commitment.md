# Character and commitment — J001–J020

These questions examine the path from broad potential to a durable specialization. Use fresh-start
development fixtures, actual resolved action records, commitment previews, and comparable mature
characters; do not infer player outcomes from a progression diagram alone.

Sources: [character development](../systems/character.md), [learning](../systems/learning.md),
[decisions](../systems/decisions.md), and [development checkpoints](../systems/checkpoints-development.md).

## Early agency and evidence

- [ ] **J001.** In a fresh character's first useful practice loop, does the same resolved action teach a relevant capability without silently committing the character to a mature identity?
  - **Evidence:** beginner fixture, learning ledger, and any state changed by the action.
  - **Falsifier:** a low-risk experiment consumes structural capacity or irreversibly closes a path.
- [ ] **J002.** When two fresh characters face the same controlled situations but take different meaningful actions, do they develop differently after equipment and random-stream effects are held constant?
  - **Evidence:** paired action traces, credited evidence, and resulting development previews.
  - **Falsifier:** the histories differ but produce the same available developments for reasons unrelated to the design.
- [ ] **J003.** Can a player try a basic physical, magical, subtle, and gathering action before an adaptation decision without needing to know a hidden build recipe?
  - **Evidence:** fresh-start route and the first adaptation opportunity in each supported activity family.
  - **Falsifier:** experimentation is blocked by a class-like starting choice or inaccessible prerequisite.
- [ ] **J004.** At an actual major-development offer, can the player trace each offered option to relevant practice and see its structural cost, incompatibilities, and foreclosed alternatives before accepting?
  - **Evidence:** offer preview, evidence provenance, and confirmation interaction.
  - **Falsifier:** an irreversible consequence appears only after acceptance or cannot be attributed to visible history.
- [ ] **J005.** In a mature-character fixture with conflicting high-tier adaptations, does finite structural capacity prevent eventual purchase of every option even after unlimited ordinary practice?
  - **Evidence:** capacity rules and an adversarial maximum-development fixture.
  - **Falsifier:** only an increasing experience price delays universal mastery.

## Commitment, branching, and identity

- [ ] **J006.** When a character develops a nearby branch—such as adding a modest ward to channeling—can it become useful without erasing the original specialization or requiring an unrelated full reset?
  - **Evidence:** branch prerequisites, available capacity, and a same-situation capability comparison.
  - **Falsifier:** any adjacent development either collapses the identity or is functionally inaccessible.
- [ ] **J007.** Can the same mature character change from distributed projectile control to concentrated close-range casting only by paying the intended structural cost, rather than swapping equipment or prepared techniques?
  - **Evidence:** controlled loadout-swap and adaptation-state fixtures.
  - **Falsifier:** a loadout change alone grants the incompatible mature expression.
- [ ] **J008.** Does the commitment interaction prevent accidental acceptance while still making deliberate acceptance possible without hidden confirmation traps?
  - **Evidence:** input-path tests for preview, defer, confirm, cancel, and reload.
  - **Falsifier:** one accidental input commits, or a legitimate choice is silently lost.
- [ ] **J009.** If a player defers an available adaptation and continues practicing, does the later offer remain understandable and bounded rather than expiring invisibly or accumulating into a generic point bank?
  - **Evidence:** deferred-offer lifecycle and development-state transitions.
  - **Falsifier:** waiting creates an unrelated purchase currency or silently removes an earned choice.
- [ ] **J010.** When a learned technique is unequipped or unprepared, do the character's foundations and prior commitments remain unchanged?
  - **Evidence:** before/after state for technique and loadout changes.
  - **Falsifier:** preparation state acts as a hidden respec or deletes development.
- [ ] **J011.** Can an enchanted or highly specialized item improve action performance without multiplying character learning or substituting for a missing structural adaptation?
  - **Evidence:** identical action histories with and without the item, including learning credit and legality.
  - **Falsifier:** the item becomes a repeatable training multiplier or grants an incompatible mature capability.

## Learning quality and temporal rules

- [ ] **J012.** Across application close/reopen and real-world waiting, does a character receive no mastery unless a qualifying in-game activity occurred?
  - **Evidence:** persisted state before and after offline intervals.
  - **Falsifier:** wall-clock time alone changes learning, proficiency, or adaptation eligibility.
- [ ] **J013.** After a long camp or a death-related world-time jump, can ecology or recovery advance without granting practice credit to the character?
  - **Evidence:** paired world-time and development records.
  - **Falsifier:** elapsed turns or recovery time are treated as meaningful practice.
- [ ] **J014.** In a repeatable safe encounter, does a shared finite opportunity cause identical low-risk actions to plateau even when the player changes weapon, reloads, or leaves and returns?
  - **Evidence:** encounter identity, opportunity claims, and repeated-resolution traces.
  - **Falsifier:** a trivial route can be reset to mint unlimited advancement.
- [ ] **J015.** If one action has several compatible discipline tags, is its bounded learning opportunity apportioned rather than multiplied once per tag?
  - **Evidence:** credited totals for a single event with one and multiple valid relationships.
  - **Falsifier:** adding labels alone increases total advancement.
- [ ] **J016.** Does a genuine failure provide at most relevant, bounded learning without making deliberate sabotage more rewarding than successful execution?
  - **Evidence:** matched success/failure fixtures and their credited contribution.
  - **Falsifier:** repeatedly failing a task is the optimal practice strategy.
- [ ] **J017.** Does a long period of not using an old skill leave it known, while current structural or equipment limits can still make it impractical?
  - **Evidence:** disuse and return fixture with saved proficiency and present legality.
  - **Falsifier:** inactivity silently decays skill, or retained knowledge bypasses present constraints.

## Endgame power and communication

- [ ] **J018.** At high refinement, can a specialist accomplish a materially larger, more controlled, or more reliable feat than their earlier self in the same situation?
  - **Evidence:** matched early/mature fixture results, not only changed descriptions or percentages.
  - **Falsifier:** every gain is canceled by a trade-off so completely that power is cosmetic.
- [ ] **J019.** When a refinement increases one dimension, such as area or sustained protection, are its competing constraints legible without reducing every advanced action to a sidegrade?
  - **Evidence:** technique preview, action resolution, and comparable alternatives.
  - **Falsifier:** the constraint is hidden, arbitrary, or nullifies the promised capability.
- [ ] **J020.** If an overall character rank is displayed, can it summarize maturity without granting generic power, replacing prerequisites, or scaling enemies?
  - **Evidence:** rank calculation and a same-world comparison across character ranks.
  - **Falsifier:** rank itself unlocks interchangeable stats or changes established foe power.
