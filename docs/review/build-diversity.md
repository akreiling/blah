# Concrete build diversity — J041–J060

These questions use the named identities as adversarial test fixtures, never as selectable classes or a
complete catalog. Compare characters through their practiced histories and actual interactions, not
only through a build label, spell list, or best-case damage number.

Sources: [build diversity](../systems/builds.md), [disciplines](../systems/disciplines.md),
[weaving](../systems/weaving.md), and the [reservoir encounter](../systems/reservoir-example.md).

## Named identities as test cases

- [ ] **J041.** Against the same reachable target and starting position, can a close-range magical blaster convert concentration and protected entry into a payoff that justifies its exposure and short reach?
  - **Evidence:** matched close-caster and ranged-caster encounter traces with approach and recovery costs.
  - **Falsifier:** the close build is always dominated by safe ranged delivery or has no supported protection route.
- [ ] **J042.** In a route with cover, moving targets, and ammunition recovery, does a shuriken specialist make decisions about trajectory, placement, and distributed attention beyond selecting a projectile attack?
  - **Evidence:** projectile state, retrieval rules, target movement, and action sequence.
  - **Falsifier:** ranged identity reduces to a damage multiplier with no meaningful delivery choice.
- [ ] **J043.** In a battlemage-thief approach, does subtle entry plus close coordination produce a distinct opportunity without requiring every peak magic, stealth, and melee specialization at once?
  - **Evidence:** practice path, commitment capacity, and a discovery-to-strike encounter fixture.
  - **Falsifier:** the hybrid is a weaker bundle of two specialists or an uncosted universal toolkit.
- [ ] **J044.** Against an unsuitable or observant target, can a poison-stealth mage remain a coherent identity through scouting, route choice, or another supported delivery instead of either automatic success or forced generic blasting?
  - **Evidence:** target susceptibility, observer signals, alternate objective, and retreat options.
  - **Falsifier:** every apex is blanket poison-immune or every target is trivially poisoned.
- [ ] **J045.** In a sustained pressure encounter, can a raging warded juggernaut use protection to enable forceful commitments while movement, sustain load, and exposed intervals remain consequential?
  - **Evidence:** action trace showing rage, ward capacity, interruption, and recovery.
  - **Falsifier:** warding erases all risk, or the character cannot express the intended physical-magical identity.
- [ ] **J046.** Against a group with multiple routes and varied cold tolerance, can an area-frost mage reshape the decision space at substantial scale without permanently denying all targets an action?
  - **Evidence:** field geometry, escape routes, upkeep, target response, and interruption.
  - **Falsifier:** area control is either inconsequential or a permanent universal lock.
- [ ] **J047.** In a constrained terrain fixture with required landing space and meaningful load, does an acrobat gain a distinct physical approach that changes position and timing rather than merely adding a dodge stat?
  - **Evidence:** movement legality, landing outcome, and resulting attack or escape opportunity.
  - **Falsifier:** acrobatic practice has no consequential action beyond generic avoidance.
- [ ] **J048.** In the same immutable encounter, do a close blaster, projectile specialist, and battlemage thief each have a viable but mechanically different opportunity?
  - **Evidence:** one encounter definition, per-build action traces, and actual capability requirements.
  - **Falsifier:** one hidden item or exact authored build is mandatory for all approaches.

## Cross-family and same-track comparisons

- [ ] **J049.** Can a physical/physical character combine practices such as Prowess and Ranged arms into a distinctive heavy thrown-spear or projectile-control tactic?
  - **Evidence:** a legal composition and an outcome not available from either component alone.
  - **Falsifier:** meaningful integration is reserved for physical/magical pairs.
- [ ] **J050.** Can a magical/magical character combine methods such as Evocation and Binding into a bounded field or other integrated effect without a weapon-track prerequisite?
  - **Evidence:** component-only and combined fixtures, including sustain and break conditions.
  - **Falsifier:** the relationship is only a named recipe or a passive multiplier.
- [ ] **J051.** Given two characters with the same discipline names but different commitments, practiced relationships, and refinements, do their available actions and limits differ materially?
  - **Evidence:** same-track character pair in a common scenario.
  - **Falsifier:** the track labels alone determine an identical kit and strategy.
- [ ] **J052.** After equalizing suitable equipment, do two characters with different earned histories still produce distinct strategies in the same encounter?
  - **Evidence:** controlled build comparison with equipment held equivalent.
  - **Falsifier:** the apparent build identity disappears when item bonuses are normalized.
- [ ] **J053.** Across the named fixtures, do shared states such as Awareness, Position, Commitment, Protection, and Environment explain interaction results consistently?
  - **Evidence:** preview and resolution logs for at least two different techniques using the same state.
  - **Falsifier:** near-identical situations produce contradictory one-off status behavior.
- [ ] **J054.** Against an apex, can a less favorable build recognize a meaningful limit or alternate objective without being promised equal win rates?
  - **Evidence:** capability mismatch, readable warning, and a supported fallback route.
  - **Falsifier:** fairness is implemented as identical effectiveness or as an opaque forced loss.
- [ ] **J055.** In a fixture with multiple threats, terrain, and resource constraints, does any one named identity fail to become the cost-free best answer to every meaningful situation?
  - **Evidence:** cross-situation comparisons that include opportunity costs, not just damage.
  - **Falsifier:** a universal solution dominates without exposure, resource, or structural trade-off.
- [ ] **J056.** If an encounter or reward is generated after a player specializes, is it independent of the player's current build and prepared techniques?
  - **Evidence:** generation inputs and paired worlds for different player states.
  - **Falsifier:** the system inspects a build and selects a bespoke counter or guaranteed matching reward.

## Discovery and replay intent

- [ ] **J057.** Can an uncoached tester find a useful, legal composition not named in the build matrix through discoverable shared rules rather than a designer-supplied recipe?
  - **Evidence:** recorded player route, actual composition legality, and interaction outcome.
  - **Falsifier:** the apparent discovery was directly prompted or required a new pair-specific rule.
- [ ] **J058.** When a player discovers a legal unlisted composition, can it earn appropriate integration practice and survive persistence without first becoming an authored named build?
  - **Evidence:** learning provenance, canonical composition record, and save/load result.
  - **Falsifier:** only enumerated examples can be developed or retained.
- [ ] **J059.** In longitudinal playtests, can players describe a plausible different next character from a capability they observed, while still valuing their current character's strengths?
  - **Evidence:** uncoached interview notes linked to the encountered mechanic and character history.
  - **Falsifier:** stated interest arises only from frustration with a broken current build or from a cosmetic swap.
- [ ] **J060.** Do implementation fixtures demonstrate that the example matrix is incomplete by design and that generic component laws, not a build whitelist, govern admission?
  - **Evidence:** catalog policy, validation tests, and an explicitly unlisted accepted composition.
  - **Falsifier:** adding each viable player identity requires adding a class or exact build entry.
