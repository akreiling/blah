# Disciplines, weaving, and emergence — J021–J040

These questions probe whether combinations arise from causal, typed rules rather than labels or an
exhaustive pairwise recipe list. Inspect the component contracts and run each relevant interaction
alone, composed, interrupted, and at its strongest legal refinement.

Sources: [disciplines](../systems/disciplines.md), [weaving](../systems/weaving.md),
[techniques](../systems/techniques.md), and [decisions](../systems/decisions.md).

## Distinct practices and shared laws

- [ ] **J021.** In a fixture where two magical methods affect the same target, do their governing constraints produce different decisions rather than merely recoloring equivalent damage?
  - **Evidence:** method rules, action costs, and observed outcomes for Evocation, Warding, Alteration, Veiling, or Binding.
  - **Falsifier:** method identity changes presentation but not valid use or trade-off.
- [ ] **J022.** For every discipline included in the implemented slice, is there both a useful ordinary application and a supported route toward a distinctive advanced expression?
  - **Evidence:** technique catalog and beginner-to-advanced fixtures for each included track.
  - **Falsifier:** a discipline exists only as a prerequisite label or late-game bonus.
- [ ] **J023.** When a physical foundation helps a related magical action, does it reduce introductory friction without granting deep proficiency in the magical discipline?
  - **Evidence:** paired character fixtures that share foundations but differ in magical practice.
  - **Falsifier:** transferable foundations substitute for substantial practice in the target discipline.
- [ ] **J024.** Does the same small interaction kernel admit meaningful physical/physical, magical/magical, and physical/magical combinations without requiring every useful weave to contain magic?
  - **Evidence:** at least one tested legal composition in each family.
  - **Falsifier:** one family has no mechanically distinct payoff or is structurally barred by a general rule.
- [ ] **J025.** For an accepted composition, can the player and core identify compatible inputs, outputs, combined costs, sustain load, and break conditions before it is used?
  - **Evidence:** component contracts, legality explanation, and resolved-action log.
  - **Falsifier:** legality depends on an uninspectable relationship or an omitted recurring cost.

## Open composition and bounded execution

- [ ] **J026.** Does the legality system derive from typed component contracts and shared interaction laws rather than requiring an exact named recipe for each pair?
  - **Evidence:** validator path for authored examples and a compatible unlisted composition.
  - **Falsifier:** unrecognized pairs fail solely because their recipe is absent.
- [ ] **J027.** If a novel combination of a recoverable carrier, decoy signal, and maintained link satisfies every component contract, can it be composed and tested without adding a bespoke “moving lure” exception?
  - **Evidence:** generic compatibility evaluation and a legal-composition fixture.
  - **Falsifier:** the result is denied despite supported inputs, capacity, and causal interaction.
- [ ] **J028.** When an otherwise plausible composition violates one typed constraint, does the legality query name the failed contract and relevant capacity?
  - **Evidence:** rejected-composition explanation for incompatible payload, range, or target.
  - **Falsifier:** rejection is an unexplained “not a recipe” or generic failure.
- [ ] **J029.** When a weave combines an initial action, sustain, and an interruptible dependency, are all resource and attention costs charged at the correct causal stage?
  - **Evidence:** cost ledger for activation, continuation, interruption, and expiry.
  - **Falsifier:** a dependent effect resolves after its required cost or support is unavailable.
- [ ] **J030.** At maximum legal composition depth and reaction count, does execution remain bounded and deterministic without excluding every unfamiliar but compatible combination?
  - **Evidence:** maximum-depth fixtures, ordering rules, and a novel legal case.
  - **Falsifier:** recursive triggers run away or the safety limit is an undocumented build whitelist.
- [ ] **J031.** Is integration learned only when two components causally contribute to one resolved outcome, rather than merely appearing on the same character or turn?
  - **Evidence:** provenance for an actual warded cast compared with an unrelated ward activation.
  - **Falsifier:** co-occurring labels earn the same credit as a demonstrated interaction.
- [ ] **J032.** When an action teaches multiple connected practices, is the learning allowance shared or bounded so that integration credit does not duplicate the whole opportunity?
  - **Evidence:** attribution totals for one outcome with several component relationships.
  - **Falsifier:** adding valid weave links multiplies total credit without limit.

## Adversarial identity fixtures

- [ ] **J033.** In a physical-only fixture, can force, ranged delivery, or acrobatic movement combine into an earned identity without an artificial magical dependency?
  - **Evidence:** a legal physical/physical composition and its practice provenance.
  - **Falsifier:** the strongest physical integration is a passive bonus or must route through a spell.
- [ ] **J034.** In a magical-only fixture, can two different methods create a useful integrated effect with real capacity and upkeep limits?
  - **Evidence:** a magical/magical composition such as bounded Evocation and Binding.
  - **Falsifier:** combining methods is either disallowed by default or grants free persistence.
- [ ] **J035.** Against a resistant or non-susceptible target, does a poison-stealth sequence distinguish valid delivery, effect susceptibility, and discovery of consequences?
  - **Evidence:** carrier, target response, observer evidence, and alternative tactical outcome.
  - **Falsifier:** stealth guarantees poison success, or a universal immunity flag is invented only to shut the build down.
- [ ] **J036.** During a raging protected surge, does Warding cover a defined part of the committed exposure while still consuming capacity and remaining breakable?
  - **Evidence:** ward load, interruption, mobility, and recovery in a pressure fixture.
  - **Falsifier:** rage arbitrarily forbids all magic, or protection removes every cost and counterplay.
- [ ] **J037.** In an area-frost fixture, are creature cold tolerance, field duration, obstruction, and terrain overlays separate supported facts?
  - **Evidence:** field state and target/environment outcomes under resistance and interruption.
  - **Falsifier:** resistance silently erases the whole field, or ordinary frost implicitly changes unsupported terrain.
- [ ] **J038.** At the strongest legal weave, do explicit constraints prevent one integration from granting unsupported range, duration, concealment, protection, and free upkeep simultaneously?
  - **Evidence:** maximum-refinement composition with all costs and outputs shown.
  - **Falsifier:** chaining individually valid components bypasses each other's limits.
- [ ] **J039.** Does a player-composed technique retain a stable canonical identity and compatible meaning across save/load and a rules-version change?
  - **Evidence:** serialized composition fixture and explicit compatibility behavior.
  - **Falsifier:** loading silently changes, deletes, or reinterprets the player's composition.
- [ ] **J040.** Does the bounded grammar prohibit arbitrary code, unbounded recursion, and external calls while still accepting a lawfully compatible combination that is absent from the example catalog?
  - **Evidence:** grammar limits plus a generated or manually authored unlisted legal case.
  - **Falsifier:** open outcomes require executable scripting, or bounds reject novelty by name alone.
