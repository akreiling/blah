# Resources, equipment, learning, and exploits — J061–J080

These questions target resource loops that can quietly erase specialization, make basic play untenable,
or award mastery for repetition rather than useful execution. Use resolved action ledgers, inventory
ownership, stable opportunity identities, and retry/save fixtures.

Sources: [learning](../systems/learning.md), [equipment](../systems/equipment.md),
[actions and recovery](../systems/actions.md), [character development](../systems/character.md), and
[validation](../systems/validation.md).

## Costs, recovery, and exploit loops

- [ ] **J061.** In matched physical and magical exertion fixtures, does Focus or the chosen resource model impose understandable costs without making one entire discipline family pay twice?
  - **Evidence:** action-cost rules, actual resource traces, and recovery opportunities.
  - **Falsifier:** one family has an unexplained cost exemption or cannot use its core identity sustainably.
- [ ] **J062.** Across activation, interruption, sustain, expiry, and recovery, are resource charges committed at the point required to prevent free dependent effects?
  - **Evidence:** per-action ledger for a multi-step weave and its interrupted variant.
  - **Falsifier:** an effect resolves without its required cost or gets charged twice on retry.
- [ ] **J063.** If a character exhausts Health, Focus, ammunition, or a required consumable away from home, is there a comprehensible recovery route that does not depend on already-spent supplies?
  - **Evidence:** depleted-resource travel fixture and minimum recovery options.
  - **Falsifier:** a basic character can become permanently stranded by a legal sequence.
- [ ] **J064.** Can any cycle of warding, healing, damage, ammunition return, or repeated effect refresh create more usable resources than its authorized sources provide?
  - **Evidence:** invariant and long-run adversarial loop fixture.
  - **Falsifier:** resources grow without a bounded world source or explicit cost.
- [ ] **J065.** In a self-injury and healing loop, does practice credit remain finite and reflect a real task rather than rewarding the player for manufacturing injury?
  - **Evidence:** repeated-loop learning totals and opportunity claims.
  - **Falsifier:** harmless self-harm is a better progression farm than meaningful healing.
- [ ] **J066.** For a delayed poison or persistent damage effect, can each tick avoid minting a new learning opportunity for the original delivery?
  - **Evidence:** event provenance and credit over the full duration, including reload.
  - **Falsifier:** one application awards fresh practice each turn without a new relevant action.
- [ ] **J067.** Does the same unresolved encounter retain spent learning allowances after reload, retreat, respawn, or a change of equipment?
  - **Evidence:** stable encounter/opportunity IDs and repeated-route results.
  - **Falsifier:** an easy reset duplicates progress.
- [ ] **J068.** When an action is requested but has no useful effect—such as firing into empty ground or striking an inert target—does the learning evaluator award no more than its declared bounded failure credit?
  - **Evidence:** requested versus resolved effect and credited amount.
  - **Falsifier:** button presses or requested magnitudes drive advancement.
- [ ] **J069.** If a depleted node or actor leaves an active cache, does revisiting after eviction preserve its depletion and learning/reward history?
  - **Evidence:** durable world record before and after cache eviction.
  - **Falsifier:** cache regeneration recreates a resource or reward that was already consumed.
- [ ] **J070.** When an item boosts a practiced technique, does it improve expression without transferring practice to the character or bypassing structural legality?
  - **Evidence:** item-on/item-off learning and legality comparison.
  - **Falsifier:** an item becomes a reusable advancement multiplier or grants forbidden identity depth.

## Fitting, rewards, and economy

- [ ] **J071.** Do structural feasibility and current fitting remain separate, so an unprepared technique is still learned while an extra slot cannot override an incompatible adaptation?
  - **Evidence:** learned-but-unprepared and structurally-incompatible loadout fixtures.
  - **Falsifier:** equipment preparation silently deletes knowledge or bypasses commitments.
- [ ] **J072.** Can a relic that changes a bounded rule support a build without becoming a replacement for its major adaptation or required practice?
  - **Evidence:** relic-only versus practiced-character comparison and item contract.
  - **Falsifier:** the relic gives full mature identity to any wearer at negligible opportunity cost.
- [ ] **J073.** For each item in a contested fixture, can the player distinguish whether it grants an effect, modifies a known technique, or changes capacity?
  - **Evidence:** catalog semantics, legality explanation, and player-facing description.
  - **Falsifier:** tooltip and actual evaluator disagree about the item's role.
- [ ] **J074.** In a guarded-cache fixture, can the player obtain meaningful treasure through an alternate route or supported capability without killing every guardian?
  - **Evidence:** reachable placements, objective validation, and reward-claim behavior.
  - **Falsifier:** the only legal path requires defeating the contents of the cache or one hidden exact key.
- [ ] **J075.** After claiming a unique relic, do stack splitting, item swaps, death, reload, and late encounter completion preserve single ownership?
  - **Evidence:** item identity and atomic reward-claim tests.
  - **Falsifier:** any supported transition duplicates the relic or loses the authoritative owner.
- [ ] **J076.** Can the gathering and equipment economy support useful non-combat growth without making exterminating a creature population the optimal source of practice or materials?
  - **Evidence:** resource provenance, finite yield, and combat/non-combat route comparison.
  - **Falsifier:** repeated kills dominate all sustainable alternatives for character growth.
- [ ] **J077.** If a consumable is required to reach a resource source, is there an alternate source or recovery path that avoids a circular dependency?
  - **Evidence:** dependency graph for supplies, access, and replenishment.
  - **Falsifier:** using the last required item makes every replenishment route impossible.
- [ ] **J078.** When an item is unsuitable for the current build, can storage or a bounded conversion preserve useful choice without secretly tailoring every reward to that build?
  - **Evidence:** reward table, ownership rules, and available non-tailored disposition.
  - **Falsifier:** every reward is either useless forever or automatically selected from current player stats.
- [ ] **J079.** At strongest legal equipment and preparation, does the character still need relevant practice and structural support for its signature capability?
  - **Evidence:** untrained high-equipment fixture and mature practiced comparison.
  - **Falsifier:** gear alone grants the full intended identity or removes every meaningful limit.
- [ ] **J080.** Can a tester turn reward retry, resource gathering, item transfer, and encounter reset into repeatable unbounded advancement or loot?
  - **Evidence:** end-to-end exploit fixture across save/load and world ownership.
  - **Falsifier:** repeated rewards or learning appear without a newly authorized source.
