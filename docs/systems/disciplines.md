# Physical and magical disciplines

[Design index](../SYSTEMS_ROADMAP.md) · [Decisions](decisions.md) · [Checkpoints](checkpoints.md)

Proposed design. See the decisions document for committed direction versus prototype rules.

## Magical disciplines: different methods, not five damage colors

This is a proposed long-term map. Five is a useful starting count, not a commitment to implement all five
before the first checkpoint. These are not copies of Hardy's named disciplines or laws.

| Discipline | What the practitioner learns | Governing constraint | Examples | Natural connections |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Evocation | Release and shape power directly | Output, reach, area, precision, and commitment draw on a defined delivery budget | Missile, close force burst, frost pulse, heat lance | Physical delivery, protection, anchors, area shaping |
| Warding | Establish boundaries and govern how effects cross them | Every ward has a supported protection rule, capacity, coverage, and sustain cost | Impact guard, protective shell, brief sanctuary, selective barrier | Rage, committed casting, healing, mobile defense |
| Alteration | Change a supported property of a substance or living target | A valid target or carrier, scope, duration, and material or effect budget are required | Fictional venom, mending, reinforcement, magical extraction | Stealth, projectiles, gathering, ward maintenance |
| Veiling | Shape what observers perceive or can locate | It changes specified signals or evidence, not the underlying physical consequences | Quiet casting, decoy, obscuration, false direction | Poison delivery, acrobatics, ranged repositioning, retreat |
| Binding | Maintain a supported relationship among actors, objects, or places | Links have anchors, range, load, duration, and break conditions | Returning weapon, anchored field, tether, maintained mark | Distributed projectiles, frost areas, traps, sustained protection |

Each discipline must have useful ordinary applications, a distinctive advanced path, and lawful interactions
with other disciplines. An element or subject is not the same as a method:

- **Frost** can be a direct evocation, a maintained bound field, or a protective ward with a specific thermal
  rule. Learning one form does not grant all the others.
- **Poison** can involve preparing an altered carrier, applying it by a physical technique, veiling the
  delivery, or binding a supported delayed trigger. There is no blanket "poison mage" class.
- **Protection** can support a physically dominant juggernaut, a subtle infiltrator, or a stationary caster,
  but the ward's mobility, coverage, and sustain requirements differ.
- **Healing** can combine alteration with protection and careful access; it is not a universal free reset
  hidden behind a generic magic score.

The laws are authored game rules. They do not require a real chemistry engine, full thermodynamics, or an
unrestricted simulation of every possible transformation.

No discipline has authority to ignore another's limits. Binding a frost field preserves its location and
duration under a supported rule; it does not make its upkeep free. Veiling a venom carrier suppresses
specified signals; it does not make the victim's injury unnoticeable by default.

## Physical disciplines: different ways of inhabiting a body

Physical development needs as much identity and depth as magical development. It cannot be a single
"weapons" track next to several interesting magical ones.

| Discipline | What the practitioner learns | Governing constraint | Examples | Natural connections |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prowess | Apply force, momentum, and controlled fury | Large force requires leverage, commitment, and recovery | Heavy blows, rushing impacts, committed surge, cleaving | Warding, close evocation, bracing, displacement |
| Endurance | Remain effective under load and threat | Holding, moving, and recovering compete for bodily capacity | Bracing, load bearing, resisting interruption, sustained effort | Rage, anchored wards, healing, expedition support |
| Acrobatics | Control movement, balance, and changing lines of approach | Routes, landing space, load, and timing constrain movement | Vault, evasive step, redirect momentum, moving attack | Close bursts, subtle movement, thrown attacks, mobile wards |
| Precision | Apply exact physical control and exploit openings | Good access, timing, and manageable simultaneous demands | Fine strikes, joint attacks, careful hands, delicate extraction | Alteration, distributed control, stealth, gathering |
| Stealthcraft | Control physical evidence and exploit observer behavior | Position, terrain, light, sound, and observer senses matter | Quiet approach, concealment, misdirection, ambush | Veiling, poison, acrobatics, ranged repositioning |
| Ranged arms | Deliver and recover physical force across distance | Trajectory, travel, ammunition, exposure, and control matter | Shuriken, bows, thrown spears, returning projectile practice | Precision, binding, altered carriers, area denial |

Weapons are instruments through which disciplines are practiced. They do not each require a duplicate
full tree. A heavy thrown spear can express Prowess and Ranged arms; close knife work can express Precision
and Stealthcraft. An acrobat can wield magic. A barbarian-like character can sustain protection.

Physical power is allowed to become extraordinary. At high mastery, it can control crowds, break supported
structures, resist tremendous force, or maintain astonishing trajectories. It must not become a mundane
starter path whose only endgame is switching to spellcasting.

## Foundations, disciplines, and delivery are independent

The layers answer different questions:

```text
foundations and adaptations: what can this person support?
disciplines and proficiency: which lawful practices have they developed?
technique structure: how is that practice expressed in this action?
integration: which practices can operate together coherently?
equipment and preparation: which supported expression is available now?
```

This permits physical/physical, magical/magical, and physical/magical weaves. Do not require a magical
discipline for every interesting physical integration, or a weapon discipline for every effective caster.

Some developments share foundations and reduce introductory friction. That is not transferable mastery:
precise sword control can help understand a careful gesture, but does not award years of Veiling or Binding
practice. One generic Channeling score cannot buy every magical discipline at full depth.
