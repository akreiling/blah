# Worked encounter: the reservoir

[Design index](../SYSTEMS_ROADMAP.md) · [Decisions](decisions.md) · [Checkpoints](checkpoints.md)

Proposed design. See the decisions document for committed direction versus prototype rules.

This is a proposed fixture, not content already supported by the baseline game.

## World facts

- A marsh region contains a water-affine, armored lineage.
- A ruined reservoir is a generated landmark with several approach placements.
- A rare individual has a validated relationship to the reservoir's magical water source.
- Ordinary relatives have a smaller, independently supported shell-defense ability.
- Temporary flooding is a core terrain overlay, not a mutation of the underlying terrain seed.

If those capabilities are not implemented or those facts do not exist in the snapshot, neither a hand-authored
fixture nor a remote draft may claim this arc is executable.

## Beat sequence

| Beat | Player experience | Mechanical purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outer marsh | Ordinary relatives are manageable or trivial for a strong character | Establish the lineage and demonstrate player power |
| Abandoned nests | Wildlife has withdrawn; water behaves unusually | Warn of a site-scale force |
| Channel guardians | Smaller shells draw water, defend, release, and expose an opening | Teach the timing relationship |
| Upper bank | A vantage point reveals channels and escape routes | Permit observation and planning |
| Reservoir guardian | The apex controls several channels and combines the same mechanics at scale | Test timing, movement, target choice, and commitment |
| Aftermath | Water stabilizes or changes course; the reward site is accessible | Record a consequence beyond a dropped loot bag |

The route does not require killing every outer creature. Lesser encounters are evidence and opportunity,
not mandatory toll booths.

## Apex mechanics

Prototype state cycle:

1. **Draw:** visible channels fill a protective shell.
2. **Hold:** protection is strongest; the apex commits to a directed threat.
3. **Release:** a telegraphed burst affects specified tiles.
4. **Exposure:** the shell is temporarily weak while water redistributes.

Supported opportunities:

- Interrupt a channel at a cost.
- Move to safe terrain before the release.
- Exploit the exposure window.
- Use protection to remain in an aggressive position.
- Approach quietly to alter a channel before alerting the apex.

The final fight can combine these at a dramatic scale without becoming an unrelated minigame.

## Different characters

**Close-range magic blaster**

- Uses mobility or protection to enter during a safe interval.
- Commits a concentrated burst during exposure.
- Risks being nearby when the next release begins.

**Long-range shuriken specialist**

- Observes channel and trajectory geometry.
- Applies pressure to exposed points or supported channel mechanisms.
- Must maintain space and recover from interrupted distributed control.

**Battlemage thief**

- Uses observation and decoys to approach a useful position.
- Quietly disrupts a supported channel or takes an ancillary objective.
- Uses a ward and close technique if discovered.

**Warded duelist**

- Holds a carefully chosen position.
- Survives a predictable portion of the release.
- Answers during a limited opening rather than chasing constant damage.

None receives a special exemption because of a build label. The same mechanics create the opportunities.

## What the draft can and cannot change

The drafter can arrange validated clues, choose among available placements, select legal beat templates,
and write supported prose.

It cannot:

- Add an unsupported immunity.
- Spawn a perfect counter to the player's prepared technique.
- Increase the apex's power because the player returned stronger.
- Claim a channel can be disrupted if no supported action affects it.
- Relocate the reward after the player has discovered its site.

## Fixture acceptance

- [ ] The warning is visible before an unavoidable lethal commitment.
- [ ] Lesser relatives teach the actual shell cycle.
- [ ] At least three test identities have distinct plausible approaches.
- [ ] The fight is not solved only by damage racing.
- [ ] The fight is not solved only by one specific immunity or item.
- [ ] A weak character can recognize danger and retreat from the warning area.
- [ ] A strong character can clear lesser relatives quickly.
- [ ] The apex remains threatening to an appropriately advanced character.
- [ ] Save/load during each phase preserves pending effects and reward state.
- [ ] Retreat and return follow the documented recovery rule.
- [ ] The aftermath and reward are durable.
